Innovation

Engineering Excellence

Patented technologies that redefine shower performance and sustainability

AirBoost™ Technology

Infuses air into water droplets for 50% more volume perception while using 30% less water. Larger, softer droplets for a luxurious feel.

ThermoLock™ Valve

Precision thermostatic cartridge maintains exact temperature within ±0.5°C. Anti-scald protection with automatic shutoff at 38°C.

SilenceFlow™

Advanced fluid dynamics reduce turbulence noise by 60%. Enjoy a peaceful, spa-like shower experience even at high pressure.

EcoSense™ Monitor

Real-time water and energy consumption display. Set daily limits, track savings, and earn eco-rewards through our app.

EasyClean™ Nozzles

Silicone nozzles resist limescale buildup. Simply wipe with a finger to restore full flow - no chemicals or tools needed.

DuraFinish™ Coating

PVD coating technology provides 10x scratch resistance. Available in Chrome, Brushed Nickel, Matte Black, Brushed Gold, and Gunmetal.
