Rain Symphony

Immerse yourself in nature's perfect rainfall

The Rain Symphony collection captures the essence of a gentle rainstorm. Each shower head is engineered with precision-drilled nozzles that create a luxurious, enveloping water curtain. Available in sizes from 200mm to 500mm, with both ceiling and wall mount options.

Our signature AirBoost™ technology infuses air into each water droplet, creating the sensation of 50% more water volume while actually using 30% less water. The result is a shower experience that feels abundant and luxurious while being environmentally responsible.

Cascade

Transform your shower into a wellness sanctuary

The Cascade collection redefines the shower as a therapeutic experience. Combining waterfall delivery, targeted body jets, chromotherapy lighting, and aromatherapy integration, Cascade creates a multi-sensory wellness ritual in your own home.

Each element is designed to work in harmony: the wide waterfall panel delivers a soothing sheet of water, while precision body jets target key muscle groups with adjustable pulsating massage. Integrated LED chromotherapy cycles through therapeutic colors, and the aromatherapy capsule infuses the steam with essential oils.

Pro Series

Precision

Architectural minimalism meets engineering excellence

The Precision collection is designed for the modern architectural bathroom. Clean lines, geometric forms, and flawless finishes define this range. Every component is engineered to the highest standards, from the ThermoLock™ thermostatic cartridge maintaining ±0.5°C accuracy to the SilenceFlow™ technology reducing noise by 60%.

Available in five PVD finishes including our exclusive Gunmetal, Precision products coordinate perfectly across rain showers, handhelds, body jets, and digital valves. The EasyFit™ installation system reduces install time to under 30 minutes.

Classic Series

Heritage

Timeless Victorian elegance with modern performance

The Heritage collection brings the grandeur of Victorian bathing into the 21st century. Cross-handle controls, exposed pipework, and traditional forms are crafted with modern materials and engineering. The ThermoLock™ thermostatic valve is concealed within period-appropriate housing, providing precise temperature control without compromising aesthetics.

Each piece is hand-finished in our workshops, available in Chrome, Brushed Nickel, and our signature Brushed Gold. The Heritage range includes rain showers, handhelds, and complete exposed systems that become the centerpiece of a traditional bathroom.
