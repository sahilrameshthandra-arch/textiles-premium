Quick Answers

What's included in a showroom appointment?

60-minute consultation with a design expert, hands-on product testing, water pressure demo, finish samples, and project planning assistance. Complimentary with purchase.

Do you offer virtual consultations?

Yes! Book a 45-minute video call. We'll send fabric swatches and finish samples beforehand. Perfect for initial planning or remote locations.

How long does a quote take?

Standard quotes within 48 hours. Complex projects (5+ bathrooms, hospitality) may take 5-7 business days for detailed specifications.

What's your warranty?

10 years on shower fixtures, 5 years on electronics/smart features, 3 years on textiles. Full details at warranty page.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes, we serve 120+ countries. International shipping quotes provided with project quotes. Local dealer support in major markets.
