Design Tips from Our Experts

Scale Your Shower

Match rain head size to ceiling height. 300mm for 2.4m, 400mm for 2.7m, 500mm for 3m+ ceilings.

Layer Your Lighting

Combine chromotherapy with warm ambient (2700K) and task lighting. Dimmers are essential.

Coordinate Finishes

Match shower hardware to faucets, towel bars, and cabinet hardware. Consistency creates luxury.

Textile Harmony

Choose towel colors that complement tile undertones. Neutral tiles = colorful towels. Patterned tiles = solid towels.

Plan for Storage

Integrate niches at shower height. Bench seating adds function and spa feel. Heated towel rails are worth it.

Water Efficiency

EcoFlow™ saves 40% water without compromising experience. Smart showers track usage for sustainability goals.
