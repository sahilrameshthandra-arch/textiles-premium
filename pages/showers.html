Shower Buying Guide

Everything you need to know to choose the perfect shower

Measure Your Space

Consider ceiling height, wall distance, and existing plumbing. Rain showers need minimum 2.1m ceiling height for optimal experience.

Check Water Pressure

Most premium showers require 1.5-3 bar pressure. Our digital showers work from 0.5 bar with built-in pumps.

Consider Your Routine

Morning rush? Quick-set digital presets. Evening unwind? Rain + body jets combo. Family? Handheld + rain dual system.

Match Your Style

Choose finishes that complement your bathroom: Chrome for classic, Matte Black for modern, Brushed Gold for luxury.
