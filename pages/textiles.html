Care Guide

Keep your textiles looking and feeling new for years

01

Wash Before First Use

Initial wash removes finishing agents and maximizes absorbency. Wash separately in warm water.

02

Use Mild Detergent

Avoid bleach, fabric softeners, and harsh chemicals. They coat fibers and reduce absorbency.

03

Tumble Dry Low

Low heat preserves fiber integrity. Remove promptly to prevent wrinkles. Air dry for maximum longevity.

04

Skip Fabric Softener

Softeners create a waxy buildup. Add ½ cup white vinegar to rinse cycle monthly to restore softness.

05

Store Properly

Ensure completely dry before storing. Avoid plastic bags. Cedar blocks naturally repel moisture and pests.

06

Rotate Your Set

Use 3 sets in rotation: one in use, one in wash, one in storage. Extends life significantly.
